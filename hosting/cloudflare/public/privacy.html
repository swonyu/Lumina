Lumina trainer

Privacy and your data

Lumina is a personal journal with local coaching. This policy describes the app’s local implementation and the choices you control.

Updated 17 November 2026 · App Store record 6773973606

Records you choose to keep

Your saved journal may contain profile details, goals, workouts, meals, check-ins, habits, journal text, selected photos, body measurements, earlier health snapshots and coaching notes. Earlier prototype versions added sample entries. Existing entries are preserved because the app cannot reliably distinguish samples from records you supplied; review their dates and contents before treating them as measurements.

Local storage and backups

Lumina stores its journal in protected app storage, including its own shared app-group storage where configured. Automatic CloudKit journal synchronization is disabled in this version.

This does not disable iOS device backups. Apple or another provider may hold copies according to your device, account and destination settings. The app does not control those external copies.

On-device coaching

When Apple’s system language model is already available and supports the selected app language, Coach uses it on your device. A request can include your question, a limited summary of saved records and excerpts from recent conversation. The request marks incomplete context to inform the model when records or conversation excerpts have been omitted. The model can still make mistakes when interpreting that context. The app does not attach photos or unverified historic AI notes, and it does not read saved API keys or other provider credentials for this request. Anything you type in the question is part of the request, so do not enter passwords or other secrets.

The current chat makes no requests to a cloud AI provider. It requires no separate model account, API key or manual model download. When the system model is unavailable, busy or too slow, the app can use general local rules and identifies that source. A model refusal is shown as an app notice. You can stop a request, and the app also cancels it when you leave Coach or put the app in the background.

Model output can be inaccurate. Coaching is intended for general reflection; do not rely on it for diagnosis, validated health measurements or determining a habit’s effects. The app does not provide photos to the model or let a reply execute record actions.

Developer access, advertising and analytics

The app does not automatically upload your journal, photos or chat to the developer. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, purchase, subscription or remote AI integration. No Lumina account is required. Information you independently choose to share through a support service is handled separately, as described below.

Photos, health and reminders

The system photo picker lets you choose an image to copy into Lumina. This version does not provide camera capture or voice-note recording through its supported screens.

Apple Health access is unavailable in this build. Earlier health snapshots may remain in the local journal. Lumina does not write or delete Apple Health records.

The optional weekly journal reminder requires notification permission and is scheduled locally. Its text is a general reminder and does not include your journal entries. You can turn it off in Lumina or disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Exporting your records

Export database backup creates a SQLite file with stored records and photo copies. You choose where to save it using the system file picker. Lumina does not automatically send the file to the developer. A destination you choose may store it on another device or with a cloud provider.

Keep the exported file private. It is not protected by a separate Lumina backup password, and someone who can open it may be able to read its contents. In-app restore is not available in this version.

Retention and deletion

Saved journal records remain until you remove them. In Settings, Delete all local data asks for confirmation before removing local journal records, saved photo copies and current coaching/session caches. Canceling keeps the records. Confirming also turns off the weekly reminder.

Local deletion does not remove exported files, device-backup copies, original images in Photos or Apple Health records. Manage those copies through the relevant system or provider. Lumina has no separate user account to delete.

Support information you share

The public technical issue tracker is hosted by GitHub. Reports and attachments submitted there are public and are handled by GitHub and the project maintainers. Do not submit health records, photos, journal text, backups, credentials or confidential privacy requests through that public channel.

This support website

These pages have no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded forms or third-party fonts. Cloudflare Workers serves this copy of the website. Cloudflare may process technical request information, including IP addresses and traffic data, to provide and secure its services, as described in its privacy policy. Visiting an external link is governed by that service’s policies.

Changes and questions

We update this policy when the app’s data practices change and show the update date on this page. See Support for current instructions and the public technical-report channel.
