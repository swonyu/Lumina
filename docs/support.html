Help with Lumina trainer

Support

Keep useful records, review what you saved, and choose the next step that suits you.

Protect your records

If Lumina cannot open its journal, restart the app before trying again. Do not delete or reinstall the app unless you have a backup you intend to keep. Reinstalling can remove local records, and Lumina does not currently offer an in-app restore tool.

Find a feature

  • Home: start a daily check-in and open tools such as Habits and Settings.
  • Workouts: log a session and open the saved-workout list.
  • Nutrition: log a meal, add an optional photo, and open the saved-meal list.
  • Coach: ask a question or open reflection prompts for your saved records.
  • Progress: save and review your selected progress photos.

Use the saved-record list to review the date, notes and values before editing or deleting an entry. Older prototype entries may include sample content; the app preserves existing records because it cannot reliably identify which ones you supplied.

How free coaching works

Lumina needs no separate AI app, API key, paid model account or subscription. When Apple’s on-device language model is already available and supports your selected app language, Coach can use it. Availability depends on the device, system settings, language and model readiness. Lumina does not ask you to download a model manually.

If the system model is unavailable, busy or too slow, the app can offer general local guidance and labels it accordingly. A model refusal is shown as an app notice. A local planning checklist is also available without AI. Use Stop to cancel an answer; leaving Coach or putting the app in the background also cancels the current request.

Generated replies can be mistaken. Coaching is intended for general reflection; do not rely on it for diagnosis, validated health measurements or determining a habit’s effects. The app does not provide photos to the model or let a reply automatically perform record actions. Review your actual records before making decisions.

Photos and health records

Use the system photo picker to choose an image to copy into Lumina. Wait for the image to finish loading before saving, or remove the selection. Deleting a saved Lumina photo does not delete the original from Photos.

This version does not offer camera capture, voice-note recording or Apple Health access through its supported screens. Previously stored health snapshots remain available until you delete them.

Weekly reminders

In Settings, turn on Weekly journal reminder and allow notifications if asked. The reminder is scheduled locally for Sunday at 7 pm. Turning it off cancels the pending weekly reminder. If notification access was denied, change the permission in iOS Settings before enabling the reminder again.

Export a backup

  1. Open Lumina’s Settings.
  2. Choose Export database backup.
  3. Choose a destination using the system file picker.

The exported SQLite file contains stored records and photo copies. It is not a readable PDF or spreadsheet, and in-app restore is not available. Keep it private: someone who can open the file may be able to read its contents. If you choose a cloud destination, that provider may hold a copy.

Delete local records

Choose Delete all local data in Settings and confirm. Canceling the confirmation keeps the records. Confirmed deletion removes local journal records, saved photo copies and current coaching/session caches; it also turns off the weekly reminder.

This does not delete original photos, Apple Health records, exported files or device-backup copies. Manage those through their original app or storage provider. There is no Lumina account to close.

Report a technical issue

The project’s public GitHub issue tracker is available for non-sensitive technical reports. Creating a report requires a GitHub account; using Lumina does not.

Describe the screen, the steps, the expected result, and your iOS and app versions. Reports are public. Do not include journal entries, health information, photos, database backups, passwords or API keys. Do not use the public tracker to send confidential privacy requests.
